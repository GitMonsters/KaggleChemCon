dqi.kmb (sha256 569863ae0fafd5f94fe91b498ec67ed0a4d96b826ced31cb0ff57c7fe6c063cb)
  Not my circuit. This is Ahmed Berat Ozer's v8 "Bezout-Schur splice" decoder, 883 qubits, redistributed byte for byte
  under its Apache-2.0 license. Lineage: the continued-fraction Schur decoder of Gu, Kemeny, Yelin and Lukin
  (arXiv:2610.22367; github.com/andigu/uncompute, tasks/dqi-reed-solomon); jday96314's competition rebuild
  (github.com/jday96314/QuantumAI, 892 qubits); Alex Chilton's 883 qubit reverse synthesis
  (kaggle.com/code/alexchilton/toffolis-go-18-3-schur-dqi-at-883-qubits); Ozer's Bezout conversion and mask projection
  splice (kaggle.com/code/ahmedberatozer/quantum-ai-df-thc-group-compression). Also carried in the avazaku, kirilltym,
  SeshuRaju and kchuang public portfolios, from which this copy was taken and re-verified: 102,400 shots on the default
  salt, PASS, cost 495,048,250,576.63, 6 value and 2 phase errors.

dfthcsos/ (submission.npz sha256 8229861b3ee22316e144c900737dfc54f7a30ea88de77ff5391f3375d050fa87, 19 ANGLE circuits; the 8.6121x C=59 merge of the 8.5735x fit described below)
